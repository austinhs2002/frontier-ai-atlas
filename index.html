Opinion / Choosing a plan

For everyday AI help, I’d start with the $20 ChatGPT plan.

After months on one large project, my take is that most people looking for everyday AI help will get the best value from Sol 6.2 at medium on the $20 plan. I’d spend more when a recurring task or a usage limit gives me a reason.

Why Sol is the default

Sol 6.2 is now a strong agentic coder, where earlier releases were shaky. It guides well across a broad range of subjects. That work used to send people back to Fable or Opus.

At medium it reaches index 52 for $0.41 per benchmark task, the cheapest configuration above 50 in the cost table.

When the $200 plan pays off

For repair work on Claude, I choose Fable before Opus. Opus leads the larger project. The Opus rows assume you keep a written record. When the lead learns something about your project and offers to document it, say yes. Every later session reads that record.

I expect a setup built this way to beat a general-purpose router such as Copilot Fusion on its own project. That is expected, not measured: no public test compares the two.

What this rests on

This page is opinion. It comes from one long MMORPG project with a C++ server and client, led by Opus 5.5 across three providers, and from the cost tables in this atlas. The Picks page records what that project logged. Your work may route differently.

The atlas covers three providers: Anthropic, OpenAI and Google DeepMind. I leave out others, such as Grok, because I prefer models with stricter cyber restrictions. That layer protects users as well as governments.
